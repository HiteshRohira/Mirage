# Changelog

## [1.1.16] — 2026-11-06

- Glass materials across the launcher and side sheets.
- Phone remote simplified to a direction pad.
- AirPlay HLS fixes for playlists, playback state and timeline updates.
- Licence texts and third-party notices bundled in the app.

## [1.1.13] — 2026-10-29

- Fixed an AirPlay connection bug affecting Safari.
- Improved mirroring decoder cleanup.
- Remote Settings button opens Control Center over any app.
- Wi-Fi and Inputs sheets in Control Center.

## [1.1.10] — 2026-10-28

- Smoother All apps, Control Center and Bluetooth panels.
- Separate APKs for 32-bit and 64-bit ARM.

## [1.1.9] — 2026-10-27

- Downloads are more than a third smaller on both ARM builds.
- Hardened AirPlay input handling and pairing.
- The app is now called Mirage, with the application ID `tv.mirage.launcher`.

## [1.1.7] — 2026-10-26

- Per-phone pairing for the phone remote, with rotating codes, limits and revocation.
- Reworked mirroring queues and audio handling.
- Your app order is kept across updates.

## [1.1.6] — 2026-10-24

- Built-in AirPlay receiver, with no separate app needed.
- TV-hosted phone remote.
- New Control Center and Appearance interface.
